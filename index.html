Astroparticle physicist and educator

Rafael Alves Batista

Junior Professor Chair at the Institut d’Astrophysique de Paris, Sorbonne Université

Rafael works across astroparticle physics, cosmology, and science education, with research spanning multimessenger astronomy, cosmic magnetic fields, dark matter, and the conditions that make life possible in the universe.

Highlights

  • Former “La Caixa” Junior Leader and Marie Curie Fellow at the Autonomous University of Madrid.
  • Past research appointments at Oxford, the University of São Paulo, and Radboud University Nijmegen.
  • PhD in Physics from the University of Hamburg, with earlier degrees from the State University of Campinas.
